Sponsor Gated Support 2.0.0 is a rebuild of the action and the start of its documentation site. Every part of the sponsor check now has test coverage, and every input, output, and setup step has a page of its own.

{/* truncate */}

## Why It Matters

The action decides who gets support and who gets their issue closed, so a wrong answer is visible to the people you care about most. Version 1 had no automated tests, and its setup lived in a single README.

Version 2.0.0 adds both.

## What's Included

### Tests for Every Decision

Test suites cover the sponsor check and inactive-thread handling, input parsing and defaults, sponsor lookup, exempt-file parsing, label filtering, and the run's success and failure paths.

### A Documentation Site

- **Overview** — How the label check, author check, and sponsor check lead to each outcome.
- **Usage** — A complete workflow with the events and permissions it needs.
- **Inputs and Outputs** — All 11 inputs with their defaults, and the `result` output.
- **Personal Access Token** — Step-by-step setup for the token that reads your sponsors.
- **Terminology** — Plain definitions for sponsors, insiders, gated labels, and the exempt file.

### One Tag for Version 2

Workflows reference the action as `mrjackyliang/sponsor-gated-support@v2` and get every 2.x release without editing their version.
